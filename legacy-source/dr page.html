Pioneering Precision in Modern Neurosurgery

Dr. Osama Mowafy is a world-renowned neurosurgeon dedicated to advancing the field of minimally invasive spinal and cranial procedures. With a career spanning over two decades, his medical philosophy centers on the marriage of industrial-grade technical precision and compassionate, patient-first care.

Specializing in neuro-oncology and restorative spinal surgery, Dr. Mowafy leverages cutting-edge diagnostic technology to ensure every intervention is as accurate and minimally disruptive as possible.
